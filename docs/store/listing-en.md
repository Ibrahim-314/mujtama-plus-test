**Name:** Mujtama+ (مجتمع بلس)
**Subtitle (30):** Arabic communities on Pi

**Full description:**
Mujtama+ is an Arabic-first social network built on Pi Network. It brings you together with people who share your interests, in a safe and respectful space.

• Communities: join groups about coding, cooking, travel and reading, or start your own with your own rules and moderators.
• A feed you control: posts from people and topics you care about, with clear controls over what you see.
• Safety first: a safety button on every post (mute, block, report), protection mode during pile-ons, and dedicated protections for minors.
• Privacy: end-to-end encrypted messages, data export and account deletion at any time, and no ad tracking.
• Pi Network: tip creators, subscribe to exclusive posts, and shop the community marketplace. Every payment goes through the official Pi window and needs your approval.
• Voice and video calls, group chats, and quiet hours for notifications.
• Arabic first, with English, French and Urdu.
• Community ambassadors who welcome newcomers.

Core features are free. Some extras are optional and paid in Pi.
